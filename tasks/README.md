# Tasks

This directory contains small, self-contained simulation tasks reflecting
typical workflows in ion transport waveform design and analysis.

Each task is designed to be reproducible and focuses on a specific aspect
of the transport pipeline: evaluation, design, comparison, and debugging.

---

## Task Overview

### 01 — Evaluate a Given Waveform
Load a provided waveform, simulate ion motion, and compute residual excitation.

---

### 02 — Design a Transport Waveform
Construct a transport trajectory (e.g., minimum-jerk) and evaluate its performance.

---

### 03 — Compare Two Waveforms
Compare different transport strategies (e.g., linear vs smooth) and quantify excitation.

---

### 04 — Debug an Excitation Spike
Analyze a waveform that produces excess excitation and identify the cause.

---

## Goal

These tasks demonstrate:

- simulation of ion transport dynamics  
- excitation analysis in time and frequency domains  
- tradeoff evaluation between waveform design choices  
- debugging of transport-induced excitation  

---

## Notes

These are simplified, public demonstrations of workflows relevant to
trapped-ion transport simulation. They are not tied to any specific
hardware implementation.
